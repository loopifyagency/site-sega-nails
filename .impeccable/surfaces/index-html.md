# Surface brief: index.html (SéGa Nails, one-page site)

## Scope and visitor mode

Persuade. Single scrolling page, French, static HTML/CSS/JS. The visitor decides to book by phone or Instagram.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints

Clients and prospects in Brest, mostly on a phone. Action: tap "Appeler" (tel:0698153005) or "Écrire sur Instagram" (@sega.nails). Proof: five client-work photos and five verbatim reviews supplied by the owner; hours and address. No prices, no invented figures; numbers shown must be derived from supplied facts (opening hours) or attributed (a client's "1 mois"). Imposed palette #464445 / #F5F5F4 / #DCCA9B. Brief demands GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis, pinned and horizontal sections, loader, marquee, counters, mask reveals, tilt cards, magnetic buttons, hide-on-scroll nav, full-screen mobile menu, reduced-motion support, LocalBusiness JSON-LD, map iframe linking to https://maps.app.goo.gl/75UGTxyaqKMyUyFe8.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is Séverine's appointment book, opened for you. The salon's one real ritual, being written into her agenda, becomes the whole page: a graphite book-cloth cover that opens on load, off-white ruled pages, gold-foil edges, a ribbon marker that tracks your reading. It refuses the beauty-salon template (pastel hero, three icon cards, stock photo grid).

OWN-WORLD: Book-cloth graphite #464445 (fine woven thread texture, gold fillet frame inset like a blind-stamped cover), agenda paper #F5F5F4 with faint ruled lines and a gold margin rule, champagne gold #DCCA9B as flat foil (never a gradient on text), gold-ink #7A6633 for gold text on paper. Display: Bodoni Moda (opsz, roman and italic) at giant scale; body Jost; La Belle Aurore only for handwritten agenda annotations. Almond-arch photo frames (the nail shape) and the French-tip "smile" curve as section edges; the hibiscus mark is drawn as geometric petals. Recognizable with all copy removed: gold fillet, almond frames, ribbon, ruled paper.

STORY: A visitor sees real nails and a real person's calendar, understands this is one careful technician in Brest (hygiene, colour advice, work that lasts, fair prices, per the reviews), believes it through verbatim client words, and books by calling or messaging. Each section is a different page of the same book.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Cover opens (rotateY from the spine) on a graphite inside-cover, gold fillet inset 24px. Left: H1 "Des ongles qui tiennent parole." at ~9rem, "tiennent" in gold italic; below it one sentence, the gold "Appeler" button (magnetic) and an outline "Écrire sur Instagram", plus a live "Ouvert · jusqu'à 19:00" chip computed in Europe/Paris. Right: three almond-framed photos staggered at different parallax depths over a huge gold line-art hibiscus. Nav top (hides on scroll down), ribbon at the right edge.

FORM: Candidate 7 of 7 on the ordered grounded list (lace sheet; perfume coffret with hot-foil; jeweller's box; polish shade chart; Breton gold embroidery on velvet; hibiscus botanical plate; appointment-book with gilt edges). Degraded roll, no challengers. seed: 20109f8176c798c3.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
