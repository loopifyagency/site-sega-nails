# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (user's choice). GSAP + ScrollTrigger and Lenis are vendored in the repo, fonts are self-hosted; no CDN dependency. Hostable anywhere static (GitHub Pages, Netlify, etc.).

## Users

Women (and some men) in and around Brest, mostly regulars and word-of-mouth referrals, who want long-lasting, hygienic nail work at fair prices from someone they can trust. They arrive on a phone, often from Instagram or a Google Maps / Google reviews search, and want to see the work, check the opening hours and book quickly. Visitors from elsewhere (one review comes from La Réunion) also use the salon on holiday.

## Product Purpose

SéGa Nails is a one-person nail salon in Brest run by Séverine Galant, a certified nail technician (prothésiste ongulaire certifiée). The website exists to turn a visitor into an appointment: by phone call or by Instagram message. Success is a tap on "Appeler" or "Écrire sur Instagram".

## Positioning

Personal, unhurried work by a single named technician who has been building her clientele since her beginnings: meticulous hygiene, nail art designed together with the client (colour advice, inspiration from experience), results that hold about a month, same-day availability for repairs, prices lower than most of Brest. A franchise or a large chain could not truthfully say any of this.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language: French only.
- Services shown on photos: full-set extensions in almond shape, French tips, lace/dentelle nail art, 3D flowers and bows, chrome/glitter, ombré (baby-boomer). No price list was provided: the site says rates are given on request and must not invent figures.
- Open decision: weekend and public-holiday hours (Toussaint) should be confirmed by the owner.
- Open decision: official logo file; the site draws its own hibiscus mark until the real file is supplied.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "SéGa Nails – Prothésiste Ongulaire Certifiée".
- Imposed colours: #464445 (graphite), #F5F5F4 (off-white), #DCCA9B (soft gold).
- Existing mark: a black hibiscus flower inside a thin gold ring, watermarked on the photos.
- Tone: warm, close, reassuring, hygiene and precision as pride points.

## Evidence on Hand

- Five client-work photos supplied by the owner (640×640): lace French with 3D roses, rose-gold glitter almond set, pastel ombré with 3D bows by a pool, classic pink-and-white French, black lace chrome set.
- Five genuine Google reviews supplied verbatim (Alyzée Allemand, Frederic Thellier, Katia Elise, Denise Le Gall, Bruno Galant). Quote them word for word, never invent others.
- Absent: prices, years of experience, client counts, certifications dates, team photos, salon interior photos. None of these may be fabricated; "chiffres clés" must be built only from facts that are in the brief.

## Product Principles

1. Appointment first: the phone and Instagram actions are always one tap away.
2. Show the work, then the person: the nail photos carry the proof.
3. Hygiene and durability are the promises, backed by the clients' own words.
4. Say only what is true: no invented prices, numbers or reviews.
5. Local and personal: Brest, one technician, a salon you can walk into.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast on the imposed palette, keyboard navigation, `prefers-reduced-motion` support, alt text on every photo, tap targets of at least 44px on mobile.
